Modifications: extracted Orion, Scorpius, Cygnus, Cassiopeia and Ursa Major; expanded consecutive HIP vertices in each source polyline into normalized undirected segment pairs. Separate polylines remain separate. No artwork or constellation boundaries are included.

These are Stellarium Modern stick figures. The IAU standardizes constellation areas and names, not a unique connecting-line figure. This dataset's license is distinct from the website's code license and the HYG star catalog's license.
